[Official ELI adopted text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng); [Publications Office record](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/dc8116a1-3fe6-11ef-865a-01aa75ed71a1/language-en).

[Official ELI adopted text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng); [Publications Office record](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/3e485e15-11bd-11e6-ba9a-01aa75ed71a1/language-en).

The raw English XHTML snapshots in `sources/*.html` were downloaded from the official Publications Office's public Cellar download handler on 24 November 2026. The complete retrieval URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in `source-lock.json`. No third-party screenshots, logos or commercial editorial commentary are incorporated.

The [EUR-Lex legal notice](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/content/legal-notice/legal-notice.html?locale=en) states that, unless otherwise specified, legal documents published in EUR-Lex may be reused for commercial and non-commercial purposes under the Commission's document reuse policy. Acknowledge the source and do not distort the original meaning. See also [Commission Decision 2011/833/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2011/833/oj/eng) and the [Publications Office copyright notice](https://op.europa.eu/en/web/about-us/legal-notices/publications-office-of-the-european-union-copyright). We do not claim that every EU publication, third-party element or logo has a blanket CC licence.

Changes made by TACITUS are explicit: XHTML paragraph whitespace is normalized; document structure and lexical patterns are extracted into JSON; record labels, vocabulary descriptions, limitations and reading questions are original annotations. Raw source XHTML is preserved unchanged. This derived graph is not an official EU publication, has no institutional endorsement, and does not replace the authentic Official Journal.

TACITUS's builder, validator, vocabulary descriptions and reading-route annotations follow the repository's software/content licensing where applicable. Source quotations and snapshots retain their separate EU source terms. Downstream users must preserve this attribution, source URLs, edition scope and the distinction between exact source content and TACITUS annotations.
