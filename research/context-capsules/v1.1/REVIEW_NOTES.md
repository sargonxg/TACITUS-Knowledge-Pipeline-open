# Revision critique and resolution

Version 1.1, 24 November 2026. This is an internal technical and scientific revision, not independent peer review.

1. **Contribution:** narrowed from a generic graph-improves-generation story to a testable portable contract connecting evidence selection, scoped interpretation/review, and reuse of work products across updates and handovers. This does not claim invention of knowledge graphs, retrieval or review metadata.
2. **Executable method:** replaced the generic utility-optimization formulation with the actual public validation, selection, whole-record packing and refusal procedure. Explained the separate App selector and byte/node/edge bounds. Stated what the declared-dependency invariants do and what missing annotations can still break.
3. **Worked case:** introduced the pinned official NIST AI 600-1 draft/final source-edition fixture. Citation-verification action MS-2.5-008 becomes MS-2.5-003 without a wording change, while the draft's MS-2.5-003 refers to another practice. The example preserves timing and suggested-guidance modality. Replay checks the captured text and exact record construction; it does not rerun PDF extraction by default or establish a generation benefit.
4. **Comparators:** added strong lexical+dense retrieval with registered fusion/reranking and full-context documents plus a carefully prepared note. Kept BM25 as an inexpensive ablation. The same-information flat packet retains all relation meanings so graph serialization is not confounded with extra annotations.
5. **Evaluation:** defined clean task completion; independent dual review, paired contrasts clustered by collection, failed attempts, coverage as well as precision, a sample-size/power gate, annotation-error cases, update/handover tasks and total construction/review/maintenance costs. Sample quantities remain provisional; no study has been run or preregistered.
6. **Evidence accounting:** preserved all six historical November 23 direct-provider probes and their mixed outcomes. Graph-associated improvement in one response and overstatement in another remain visible; no average causal or App-route claim follows.
7. **Publication discipline:** new immutable-version directory, dated metadata, new canonical version URL, 21-reference CSL bibliography, BibTeX, readable HTML/MathML, academic PDF, two original vector diagrams, three tables, one estimand equation, replay and checksum verification. Version 1.0 is unchanged.

Remaining scientific limitations: the contract's practical novelty and value require comparison against capable existing systems; annotations can consistently propagate an error; human-review authority and cost are unresolved deployment questions; the public source does not reproduce the whole private App; literature selection is targeted; and historical compatibility probes plus source-assembly correctness do not establish efficacy or willingness to pay.
